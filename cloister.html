Delay = (distance from the mains to the tuning point − distance from this speaker to the same point) ÷ 343 m/s, plus 8 ms so the stage is heard first. Distances are 3D to ear height (1.6 m). Tilt = angle to the aim point at ear height. Start values only: final delay and EQ come from the measurement mic.

    Tuning map design, layout method and acoustic model © 2026 Uzo Udu, UZO Media Productions. All rights reserved.

    Engine: TuneiT Space logic (speaker library with real coverage and level, 3D aim, crowd, timing and auto-trim, TuneiT score). Air 24 °C, 70% humidity (Miami at night), sound at 345.8 m/s.

    The heatmap is a simplified direct-sound model: distance loss, speaker pattern, about 6 dB lost through the arcade into a gallery, and nothing for reflections. With Crowd on, people soak up the high end along every path that runs through them below about 2.1 m (rule of thumb, 0.5 dB per metre at 1 person per m²; three quarters of the people in the courtyard, a quarter moving through the galleries). Use it to compare options, then trust the measurement mic. Gallery cells are judged against their own target, 14 dB below FOH. Weights and tower loads are estimates to confirm with FPS.
