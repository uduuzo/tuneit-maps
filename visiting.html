Delay = (distance from the mains to the tuning point − distance from this speaker to the same point) ÷ 343 m/s, plus 8 ms so the stage is heard first. Distances are 3D to ear height (1.6 m).     Tuning map design, layout method and acoustic model © 2026 Uzo Udu, UZO Media Productions. All rights reserved.

    Engine: TuneiT Space logic (speaker library with real coverage and level, 3D aim, crowd, timing and auto-trim, TuneiT score). Air 24 °C, 70% humidity (Miami at night), sound at 345.8 m/s.

    The heatmap is a simplified direct-sound model: distance loss, speaker pattern and height, and, with Crowd on, the high end people soak up along every path that runs through them below about 2.1 m (rule of thumb, 0.5 dB per metre at 1 person per m²). Trees and reflections are ignored. The sub map adds the subs by phase at 50, 63 and 80 Hz so split-sub dead zones show. Colours compare each spot with the dance-floor average. Use it to compare options, then trust the measurement mic.

    Gear from the FPS quote for the Visiting Stage: JBL VRX system (4 line-array tops, 2 subs, 4 SRX: 2 as stage monitors, 2 as ambience fillers; Yamaha QL3), Shure QLX-D, 4 × CDJ-3000 + DJM-V10, 4.88 m round stage with 3.05 / 2.44 / 1.52 m semicircle arches.
